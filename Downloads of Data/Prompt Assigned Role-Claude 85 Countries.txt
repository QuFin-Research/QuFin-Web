## Updated & New Binding Rules (Rule H Extension & Rule I)

### Rule H (Updated): FRED Primary Source Priority & Country-Specific Authentic Fallbacks
1. **FRED/ALFRED Precedence:** Whenever a macroeconomic indicator is available on **FRED/ALFRED** with **Seasonally Adjusted (`SA`)** values and **vintage revision history**, **FRED must be used as the sole canonical source**.
2. **UK (`GBR`) Official ONS Fallback (`https://www.ons.gov.uk/`):** For United Kingdom (`GBR`) macroeconomic series (such as UK Unemployment Rate `MGSX`, UK CPIH/CPI `L522`/`D7BT`, UK GDP QoQ `IHYQ`, Retail Sales, or Average Weekly Earnings) where **FRED lacks `SA` + vintage data**, use the **UK Office for National Statistics (`ons.gov.uk` free keyless API / time-series data endpoint)** as the primary authentic fallback before OECD or IMF.
3. **Strict No-Duplication Guardrail:** Never duplicate the same indicator/country pair across multiple sources.

### Rule I (New): Deep Historical Training Horizon (`1990-01-01` Floor)
1. **Historical Depth Mandate:** To train QuFin's quantitative models on full economic cycles, every newly ingested series **must fetch the deepest available vintage history starting from `1990-01-01`** (or at minimum **`2000-01-01`** where 1990s data does not exist at the source).
2. **Do Not Re-Download Existing Series:** Leave already-populated series untouched unless adding missing Sprint 1–4 coverage.
